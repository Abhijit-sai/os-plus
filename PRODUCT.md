# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Owners, managers, front-desk staff, production coordinators, workers, and finance staff at boutiques and other order-to-production businesses. They use OS PLUS throughout the working day to move customer orders from intake through production, delivery, and payment without losing operational context.

## Product Purpose

OS PLUS is a multi-tenant WorkOS that keeps the commercial order, its independently tracked production items, customer context, workflow execution, and financial state consistent. Success means routine operational work is fast and explicit while tenant boundaries, production history, payment history, and unsaved user input remain protected.

## Brand Personality

Calm, dependable, and practical. The interface should feel like a trustworthy work tool: familiar enough to use under pressure, clear about system state, and restrained in its visual language.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboard styling, novelty controls, excessive motion, hidden state changes, ambiguous save behavior, and dense desktop-only forms. Do not make the product feel like a generic analytics template or expose internal production detail on customer-facing surfaces.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the tool subordinate to the operational task.
2. Make pending, success, error, and blocked states unmistakable.
3. Preserve user-entered work and historical records by default.
4. Treat each order item as an independent production unit while keeping commercial totals coherent at the order level.
5. Use familiar, mobile-friendly interaction patterns and progressive disclosure for complex forms.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use visible labels, keyboard-accessible focus states, semantic controls, readable contrast, and touch targets suitable for mobile operation. Do not rely on color, hover, or motion alone to communicate state; respect reduced-motion preferences.
